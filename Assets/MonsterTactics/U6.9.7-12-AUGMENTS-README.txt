MonsterTactics U6.9.7 — 12 approved augments
=======================================
Base: U6.9.6 Supply Drop Augments + U6.9.6a CS0117 enum fix + U6.9.6b Damage Amp inspector patch.

INSTALLATION (PATCH)
1. Back up your Unity project and exit Play Mode.
2. Extract this PATCH archive to the Unity project ROOT (the folder containing Assets).
   Do not drag only the C# files; preserve their exact subfolders and .meta files.
3. Wait for Unity to finish compiling; fix any Console errors before continuing.
4. Menu: MonsterTactics > Augments > U6.9.7 Install 12 Approved Augments.
5. Menu: MonsterTactics > Tools > U6.9.7 Verify 12 Approved Augments.
6. Enter Play Mode and verify each effect in actual PvP/PvE combat and planning phases.

INSTALLATION (FULL)
The FULL archive has a top-level MonsterTactics folder intended to go INSIDE the Unity project's
Assets folder. Back up first; merge into Assets and overwrite matching files.

New augment definitions are CREATED by the Editor installer, not supplied as prewritten
.asset files. The installer is idempotent: rerunning it preserves any existing per-asset
Inspector changes, catalog references, enable toggles, custom description/art, and weights.
Newly created approved augment definitions start ENABLED. Disable individually as desired.

ROOKIE (5)
Weakness Policy         First drop below 50% HP: +12% AD and +12 AP rest of combat (once/unit).
Creature Station Visit       2 lowest-max-HP teammates gain a 12%-of-max-HP shield for 8 seconds.
Training Grounds        1-star Monster +12% Attack Speed and +100 HP.
Field Supplies          +4 Gold and 1 physical Component Anvil (choose 1 of 4).
Type Research           Each distinct ACTIVE elemental Type synergy grants team +3 Armor/MR,
                        capped at +12 each. Origin traits don't count.

ACE (3)
Rare Candy Cache        +8 XP immediately. NEXT 3 player level-ups grant +2 Gold each;
                        levels caused by the initial XP don't count.
Second Wind             On first drop below 40% HP, heal 12% max HP over 3 sec (once/unit/fight),
                        healing is affected by Wounds and overtime healing reduction.
Technical Machine       Units holding at least one completed item: +14% AS and +10 AP.

CHAMPION (4)
Master Ball Delivery    1 random 1-star 5-cost Monster, +12 Gold, 1 physical Complete Item Anvil.
                        5-cost reward remains pending while Monster bench is full.
Final Form Fury         2-star +15% Damage Amp/+150 HP; 3-star +30% Damage Amp/+350 HP.
Rainbow League          >=5 different fielded elemental types: team +18% Damage Amp/+15% HP.
Elite Four Sponsorship  +5 Gold immediately; every 4 PvP victories AFTER choosing it drops
                        1 physical Completed Item Anvil. PvE victories do not count.

Excluded per user's selection: Baton Pass, Expedition Contract, Revival Herb.

IMPORTANT INTERACTION
All physical anvils drop onto the board, must be picked up onto Monster bench,
are opened by dragging to the shop/Sell area, and send selected item into Item Bench.
Anvils remain on board when the 9-slot Monster reserve bench is full.

Balances are initial design values, not playtested balance.
Unity compilation and Play Mode verification must be run locally. Archive + source structural
checks do NOT prove there are no Unity compile or gameplay issues.
