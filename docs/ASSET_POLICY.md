# Art and distribution notes

This portfolio export contains procedurally produced geometric characters, icon symbols, retextured generic materials, and new static low-poly training dummies. Pixel colors and shapes were generated from deterministic filename hashes rather than transformed from the original image pixels. These are temporary visuals, not commercially finalized artwork.

- All PNGs included in the original supplied Unity ZIP were regenerated in-place.
- The original four imported 3D models have been replaced with simple COLLADA models, **without the prior rigs**.
- Archived ZIP backups containing old art, historical content snapshots, and original art-downloading scripts were removed.
- Species and trainer labels, filenames, identifiers and game-facing descriptions were mechanically rebranded; some generic historical comments may remain.
- The old characters and third-party imagery have not been intentionally included in this exported copy, but this is **not a complete intellectual-property audit or legal clearance**.
- The user must confirm that the C# source, other dependencies, sound, VFX, and all other materials may be shared, and review the result in Unity before publishing it publicly.

No original Nintendo/Game Freak artwork or 3D model bytes were intentionally carried into this public-facing copy. The Unity C# gameplay code was derived from the supplied user project and may retain game-design similarities. Licensing remains with the appropriate rights holders.
